The Mandelbrot set and the real orbit diagram, aligned on c

Same horizontal axis, top to bottom: the top panel is $M$ in the complex $c$-plane; the bottom panel is the ordinary bifurcation diagram for $x \mapsto x^2+c$ on the real line, plotted at the same real values of $c$ directly underneath. Drag a box on either panel to zoom — the other panel's $c$-range zooms with it, so you can chase a bulb boundary in $M$ and watch the matching bifurcation happen underneath, or vice versa.
