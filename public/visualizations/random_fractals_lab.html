Random Fractals Lab

Two ways to build a fractal curve from H/T bends: deterministically — the same rule every time, like always-H or alternating — or stochastically — a coin flip at every segment, and the coin doesn't have to be fair. On top of that, H and T don't have to bend by the same angle.

The takeaway

An iterated deterministic rule can produce a fractal — always-H with matched angles is just the Koch-style curve you've already seen. But an iterated random rule can produce a fractal too, and changing the coin's bias or making the two angles uneven opens up a much wider family of shapes than either fixed rule alone.
