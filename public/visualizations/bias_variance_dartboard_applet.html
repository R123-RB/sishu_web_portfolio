🧠 Connect the picture to machine learning

Bias describes systematic error: predictions are consistently shifted away from the true value.
Variance describes sensitivity to the data: predictions change a lot across different training samples.
Low bias + low variance is the goal: predictions are accurate on average and consistent. In real machine learning, bias and variance are not literally dart coordinates; the dartboard is an analogy to build intuition.
