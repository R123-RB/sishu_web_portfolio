EMBEDDED QUADRATIC JULIA SETS — z³ + λ/z³ (Plates 28–29)

At these two special values of $\lambda$, the Julia set of the cubic rational map $f_\lambda(z)=z^3+\lambda/z^3$ contains infinitely many small copies of a specific quadratic Julia set, repeated over and over at every scale. Drag to zoom into any of the small black clusters near the boundary — each one is a shrunken copy of the basilica or the Douady rabbit.
