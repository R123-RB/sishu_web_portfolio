Notes

1. Some filled Julia sets look like several separate blobs — but they can actually be joined at a single, thin point, not truly disconnected. If a shape looks pinched, that's worth a closer look before you call it "shattered."

2. Every render here scans the square from −2 to 2 in both directions, since any point outside that range is guaranteed to escape whenever |c| ≤ 2 — which covers every c-value on this page.
