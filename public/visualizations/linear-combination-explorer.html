The dashed lines are the parallelogram law from the figure: go a·v₁, then b·v₂, and you arrive at a·v₁ + b·v₂. Swapping the order traces the other two sides, which is why the shape closes.

While the two vectors point in different directions, every point in the plane has exactly one recipe. Line them up and the recipes collapse onto a single line.
