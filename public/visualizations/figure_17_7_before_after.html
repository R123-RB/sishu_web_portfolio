Watching ±b split apart — the continuous version of Figure 17.7

The two marked points are the genuine period-2 cycle, solving $z^2+z+(c+1)=0$. Drag $c$ across $-3/4$ and watch them go from a close complex-conjugate pair (left picture in the book) to two separated real points, one per lobe (right picture) — the exact instant they collide is the pinch that splits the Julia set in two.
